This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of employee attrition within an organization. The visualizations offer insights into trends by age, gender, department, education, and job satisfaction to help HR teams understand and manage workforce attrition effectively.

## Dashboard Overview
Key Metrics
- Average Age: 37 years
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%

 ## Dashboard Components
1. Department-Wise Attrition
Visualized as a pie chart.

Sales department shows the highest attrition (133 employees).

2. Age Distribution
Histogram of employee count by age bins (20 to 60).

Peak employee age group: 30-34 years.

3. Attrition by Gender
Male: 150 attritions

Female: 87 attritions

4. Education Field Attrition
Life Sciences has the highest attrition (89).

Fields include: HR, Marketing, Medical, Technical Degree, etc.

5. Job Satisfaction Rating
Matrix showing satisfaction levels (1 to 4) across job roles.

Sales Executive and Research Scientist have higher ratings at levels 3 & 4.

6. Attrition by Age & Gender
Donut charts for age bands:
- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Over 55
Percentage-wise attrition shown with total attritions per group.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau for interactive data visualization.
- Data Sources: Employee data containing demographics, job details, satisfaction ratings, etc.

 ## How to Use
- Open the .twbx Tableau file in Tableau Desktop.
- Explore individual charts or filters to gain focused insights.
- Use filters (e.g., gender, department, education field) to segment data as needed.
